Source repository: https://github.com/imgmongelli/airlines-logos-dataset

Imported revision: `7b001fb8d5d0a2f875d57b2b5a8a8056b2fbc63a`

The upstream README describes the **repository** as MIT-licensed and says its
airline records draw on publicly available IATA and ICAO sources. This does not
independently establish redistribution permission from every airline or other
copyright/trademark holder for each included logo.
Keepraw Fly imports only the logo files required by its local airline dataset,
renames them to their two-character IATA codes, and keeps them as PNG files.
At the 2026-10-10 license review, 408 files in `assets/airlines/` have identical
Git blob hashes to 408 copies in `apps/web/public/assets/airlines/`.
No upstream service is contacted at runtime.

Airline names, logos and trademarks remain the property of their respective
rights holders. They are used for airline identification, but this description does not
guarantee that each underlying image may be redistributed. See
`docs/license-review-2026-10-10.md` for the outstanding rights review.
