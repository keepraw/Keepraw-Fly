# imgmongelli/airlines-logos-dataset notice

Source repository: https://github.com/imgmongelli/airlines-logos-dataset

Imported revision: `7b001fb8d5d0a2f875d57b2b5a8a8056b2fbc63a`

The upstream README declares the repository MIT-licensed and describes its
airline records as sourced from publicly available IATA and ICAO databases.
Keepraw Fly imports only the logo files required by its local airline dataset,
renames them to their two-character IATA codes, and keeps them as PNG files.
No upstream service is contacted at runtime.

Airline names, logos and trademarks remain the property of their respective
rights holders. They are used only for airline identification.
