This dedication does not change third-party licenses or confer rights in marks.

Reference airport names, positions, and code observations draw on the
public-domain OurAirports CSV. Timezone attributes draw on the MIT-licensed
airportsdata inventory. Its notice, including the mwgg attribution, is in
LICENSE-airportsdata.txt. The datasets/airport-codes historical observations
retain ODC-PDDL-1.0 attribution and their public-domain OurAirports origin.

`LicenseRef-Reference-Only` means no open redistribution license was established.
It is not a permission grant. Source access/review status is separate from its
license: reading a copyrighted source does not make it CC0. The GAO federal report
is identified separately; embedded third-party content is not covered by that
identification. If importing further third-party data, verify its actual terms
and preserve attribution/license notices before including it.

The FAA and NOAA/NWS identifier directories, airspace orders, Federal
Register works and weather service notices retain issuer attribution as
U.S. federal works. Embedded third-party material retains its own terms.
